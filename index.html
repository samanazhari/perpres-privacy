Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The Perpres PBJ Konsolidasi application respects your privacy. This application is designed as a document reader tool that operates fully offline without collecting any user data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

This application does not collect, store, record, or share any personal information or device data. All regulatory data is stored locally within the application database.

2. Device Permissions

This application does not request or require any sensitive permissions, such as location, contacts, camera, or external storage access on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

This application does not use any third-party SDKs, trackers, analytics services, or advertising networks that monitor your digital activity.

4. Government Content Disclaimer

This application is an independent legal document consolidation tool developed for educational purposes. This application is NOT an official app of the National Public Procurement Agency (LKPP), the State Secretariat, or any government institution. The source of the original text refers to official public documents from the JDIH network.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the official email provided on the Google Play Store developer profile.
